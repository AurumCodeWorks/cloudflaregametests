How this machine plays

One press is one spin: there is no autoplay, and no way to speed a spin up or stop the reels early. Each spin takes at least 2.5 seconds.

If a spin returns your stake or less, it is shown as a return, not a win: no fanfare.

Provably fair spins

Before you spin, the server locks in a secret seed and shows you its fingerprint. Your browser adds a seed of its own. Each spin is worked out from both seeds and a counter, so neither side can choose a result.

When you reveal the server seed, your browser re-plays every spin made with it and checks each one. The server then moves to the next seed, whose fingerprint you were already shown.
